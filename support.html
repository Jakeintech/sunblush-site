The forecast

What do the verdicts mean?

Every sunrise and sunset gets one of five words: Muted, Soft, Good, Vivid or Spectacular. Vivid and Spectacular mean color is likely and it is worth going out. Tap the verdict on the Today tab to see the reasons, the cloud cover by height and the outlook for the days ahead.

Why did the verdict change during the day?

Forecasts get better as the hour gets closer, so Sunblush updates its verdict. The label under the verdict tells you how settled it is: "Early read", "Firming up" or "Latest read".

The verdict was wrong. What now?

It happens. Clouds can move in the last half hour and no forecast catches that every time. After each golden hour, Sunblush asks "How was it?" in the sky sheet. Your answers build a tally of how often the forecast matched what you saw.

It says the forecast is unavailable.

The forecast comes from Apple Weather and needs a connection. If Sunblush cannot reach it, it says so instead of guessing. Sun times still work offline. Open the app again when you are back online.

Alerts

I did not get an alert tonight.

By default Sunblush only alerts you when the forecast is Vivid or Spectacular, so a quiet evening means the sky was not expected to be special. To get an alert for every golden hour, turn off "Only for Promising Skies" in Settings. Also check that notifications are allowed in Settings > Notifications > Sunblush.

Can I choose sunrise or sunset only?

Yes. In Settings you can switch sunrise alerts and sunset alerts on or off separately, and set the alert to arrive 10, 30 or 60 minutes before golden hour.

Camera, photos and sharing

Where are my photos stored?

On your device, inside Sunblush. If you allow it, Sunblush also saves a copy to your Photos library. Nothing is uploaded.

How do I make a share card?

Open the sky sheet from the Today tab and tap "Share this sky", or open a photo and tap Share. Pick Story, portrait or square, choose a style, then send it to Instagram or save it to Photos.

Why does a share card ask for Health or Calendar access?

Only if you turn on the optional activity or calendar badges for a card. Sunblush reads the data on your device to draw the badge and does not store or send it. You can leave both off.

Widgets, Siri and Apple Watch

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Sunblush. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize and add a Sunblush widget beside the clock.

The widget shows old information.

iOS decides how often widgets refresh. Opening Sunblush updates them straight away. Keeping Background App Refresh on for Sunblush helps them stay current.

What can I ask Siri?

"How's the sky in Sunblush?", "When is golden hour in Sunblush?", "Open Sunblush camera" and "What's my Sunblush streak?"

What does the Apple Watch app show?

A countdown to the next golden hour, sun times and the moon, with complications for your watch face. The sky verdict is shown on iPhone and iPad.

Sunlight Reports

What is a Sunlight Report?

It shows when direct sun reaches a place you choose, such as a balcony or a garden, across a range of dates. You set the direction the place faces and can sketch buildings or trees that block the sun. Find it in Settings under Tools, or touch and hold a saved place.

Does the report include weather?

No. It is calculated from the sun's path and assumes clear skies. It is an estimate to help you compare places and seasons, not a survey or a guarantee.

Sunblush Pro and billing

What does Pro add?

The full three-day sky outlook, RAW capture, the Golden Hour widget in every size, unlimited saved places, full Sunlight Reports with PDF export, and the whole year ahead in the Light Chart. In the United States it is $2.99 a month, $14.99 a year with a one-week free trial, or $39.99 once.

How do I cancel a subscription or free trial?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Sunblush Pro, then Cancel. If you cancel a free trial before it ends you are not charged.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open Sunblush, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy and your data

Why does Sunblush need my location?

Sun times and the forecast depend on exactly where you are. Your location is used on your device, and its coordinates are sent to Apple Weather to fetch the forecast. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual photos in the app, or delete Sunblush to remove everything it stored. Copies saved to your Photos library stay until you remove them in Photos.
