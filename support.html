Frequently asked questions and help

What exactly does Sunblush do?

Sunblush calculates the precise golden-hour window for your location each day, nudges you when it's about to begin, and gives you a minimal one-tap camera to capture the moment. Every photo is filed in an on-device archive with its solar phase, weather, and location, and at the end of the month Sunblush hands you a recap of everything you caught.

Why does Sunblush need my location?

Golden hour depends on exactly where you are — a latitude-accurate calculation is the whole point. Location is used only to compute sunrise, sunset, golden-hour, and blue-hour times and to label your captures with a place name. It never leaves your device. You can deny or revoke access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Sunblush.

Where are my photos stored?

Inside the app, photos live in an App Group container on your iPhone — nothing is uploaded. If you grant access to the Photos library, Sunblush can also save a copy to your camera roll so your sunsets mix back into the rest of your photos. You can revoke that at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Sunblush.

Is there a social feed or community?

No. Sunblush is deliberately not a social app — no followers, no likes, no feed. The archive is personal. Sharing means exporting a photo or a recap to whatever app you already use.

Can I edit or filter the photos?

No, and that's on purpose. Sunblush is a capture tool, not an editor. The sky is already the subject; the native camera handles image quality. Authenticity is part of the archive.

Does Sunblush work without an internet connection?

Yes. Solar calculations are mathematical — they work offline for any date and location. The only feature that needs the network is the optional weather tag on each capture, which fetches current conditions from Apple's WeatherKit.

What's in Sunblush Pro?

Sunblush Pro unlocks unlimited captures, full archive history, and the monthly recap. It's $1.99 per month or $14.99 per year, and the annual plan includes a 1-week free trial for eligible new subscribers. Subscriptions are billed and managed through your Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions. Find Sunblush Pro in the list and choose Cancel Subscription. If you cancel a free trial before it ends, you will not be charged.

I missed a golden hour — can I backfill?

Captures are live only. Once the window ends, the camera returns to standby and you'll see the countdown to the next one. Blue-hour and civil-twilight adjacencies are also supported, so you have a wider window than just the 20 minutes before sunset.

How do I add the widget?

Long-press an empty area of your home or lock screen, tap the plus icon, search for Sunblush, and pick the widget size you want. The widget shows the countdown to the next golden hour and doesn't need the app to be open.

How do I delete my data?

Your archive and preferences live on your device. You can delete individual captures or clear the entire archive from within the app, and uninstalling Sunblush removes the App Group container and all locally stored data. Copies saved to the iOS Photos library stay there and can be removed from the Photos app.
