Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

The short version.

  • Sunblush has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking software.
  • Your photos and your journal stay on your device unless you choose to share them.
  • Your location is used on your device, and its coordinates are sent to Apple to fetch the weather forecast and place names. It is not sent to us.
  • We do not run a server that receives your data, and we do not sell or share personal information.

1. Who we are

Sunblush is an app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch made by Jake Williams ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes and the choices you have. Questions: info@jakeawilliams.com.

On the App Store, Sunblush's privacy label lists one item, Precise Location, used for App Functionality, not linked to you and not used for tracking. It is listed because the forecast request to Apple Weather includes your coordinates.

3. Location

With your permission, Sunblush uses your location to work out sun times for where you are, to request the forecast for that spot, and to label photos with a place name. You can refuse or withdraw permission at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Sunblush. Without it, the app uses a default city, and you can still save places by searching for them.

4. Weather and maps

Forecasts come from Apple Weather (WeatherKit). Each request contains the coordinates of your location or a saved place and nothing that identifies you to us. Place search, map display and place names use Apple Maps. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy. The sky verdict itself is calculated on your device from the forecast.

5. Photos and sharing

Photos you take in Sunblush are stored on your device with their details: the time, the place, the sun's position and the weather. Sunblush can save a copy to your Photos library if you allow it. When you share a photo or a card, it goes to the app you pick, such as Instagram or Messages, and that app's own privacy policy applies from there. Sunblush itself uploads nothing.

6. Health and Calendar

These are optional and off until you turn them on in the share card editor. Sunblush reads your activity summary, workouts or the number of calendar events for a single day in order to draw a badge on a card you are making. It does not write to Health or Calendar, does not keep the data, and does not send it anywhere. Health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone. You can withdraw access in Settings > Privacy & Security.

7. Notifications

Golden hour alerts are scheduled on your device. No push notification server is involved, and we do not know when or whether an alert was delivered or opened.

8. Purchases

Sunblush Pro is sold by Apple as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription or a one-time purchase. Apple processes the payment and tells the app on your device whether Pro is unlocked. We do not see your card number, billing address or Apple Account.

9. What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information.

Besides the Apple services above, the app makes one other network request: it downloads a small settings file from sunblush.app that lets us switch a feature off if something goes wrong. That request sends no information about you.

10. Keeping and deleting your data

Your journal, places, reports and settings stay on your device until you delete them. You can delete individual photos in the app. Deleting Sunblush removes everything it stored. Copies you saved to your Photos library stay there until you remove them in Photos. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

11. Children

Sunblush is rated 4+ and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. Because your data is stored on your own device, you can do these things directly in the app. We do not sell personal information. If you have a request we can help with, write to info@jakeawilliams.com.

13. Security

Sunblush relies on the protections built into iOS: its data sits inside the app's sandbox, protected by your passcode and the device's encryption, and every network request uses HTTPS.

14. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. For a significant change we will also say so in the app.
