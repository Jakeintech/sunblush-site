Sunblush Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 16, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Sunblush ("we," "us," or "the app"), developed by Jake Williams, handles information when you use the Sunblush iOS application. Sunblush is designed around a privacy-first architecture: the data that matters to you — your photos, your location history, your capture archive — is processed and stored on your device. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how we keep it protected.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. On-Device Data

Sunblush runs on Apple frameworks that keep your data local by design. The following happens entirely on your iPhone:

  • Solar calculations (sunrise, sunset, golden hour, blue hour) from your current location using CoreLocation.
  • Reverse geocoding to label photos with a place name.
  • Photo capture through AVFoundation and storage in an App Group container shared only between the Sunblush app and its widget.
  • Photo metadata — solar position, phase, weather snapshot, timestamp, location — stored locally with SwiftData.
  • Monthly recaps and streak tracking computed from the on-device archive.

No photo, location fix, or archive entry is transmitted off the device. Sunblush does not have an account system, and there is no server to receive your data even if we wanted one.

4. Location

With your permission, Sunblush uses precise location through CoreLocation to calculate accurate golden-hour times for where you actually are, and to label each captured photo with the place it was taken. Location is used only for these features. You can deny or revoke location access at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security > Location Services > Sunblush. Without location access, the app will fall back to a default city and will not label photos with places.

5. Photos

Photos you capture in Sunblush are written to the app's App Group container as HEIC files, alongside a SwiftData record of their metadata. With your permission, Sunblush can also save a copy to the iOS Photos library so your golden-hour shots appear alongside the rest of your camera roll. Access to the Photos library is governed by iOS; you can revoke it at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Sunblush. Sunblush never transmits photos off the device.

6. Weather

Sunblush uses Apple's WeatherKit to fetch the current conditions at the time of capture and store them as metadata on the photo (for example, "partly cloudy, 68°F"). Weather queries are coarse — they use only the location required to return the forecast — and they are not combined with any advertising identifier.

7. Subscriptions

Sunblush is free to download and use. An optional paid subscription, Sunblush Pro, unlocks premium features:

  • Sunblush Pro Monthly — $1.99 per month.
  • Sunblush Pro Annual — $14.99 per year, with a 1-week free trial for new subscribers.

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple through the App Store using your Apple ID. Sunblush receives only the transaction receipt needed to validate entitlement — we do not see your credit card number, billing address, or other payment details. Refer to Apple's privacy policy for information about how Apple handles payment data.

8. Third-Party Services

Sunblush does not bundle third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs beyond those provided by Apple. The app makes no network calls other than WeatherKit requests to Apple, and no other service receives any data about you or your captures.

9. Children's Privacy

Sunblush is rated 4+ and is appropriate for all ages, but we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at info@jakeawilliams.com and we will take appropriate action. This policy is intended to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

10. International Users (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal data, and the right to data portability. Because Sunblush stores your captures and location history on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly within the app by viewing, editing, or deleting your data.

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) gives you the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete it, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal information as defined by the CCPA.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@jakeawilliams.com.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your capture archive and preferences live on your device, they persist until you delete them. You can delete individual photos, clear your entire archive, or remove the app. Uninstalling Sunblush removes the App Group container and all locally stored data. Copies you chose to save to the iOS Photos library remain there and can be removed from the Photos app.

12. Security

We rely on the security protections built into iOS: data is stored inside the app's sandbox and App Group container, protected by the device passcode and hardware encryption. Network requests use HTTPS with certificate validation. No system is perfectly secure, but we design Sunblush to minimize the data that could ever be exposed.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, for material changes, provide notice inside the app. Your continued use of Sunblush after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
