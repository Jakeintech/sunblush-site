2. What Sunblush does

Sunblush forecasts how colorful an upcoming sunrise or sunset is likely to be, tells you when golden hour is, and gives you a camera and a journal for the skies you catch. It includes:

  • Sky forecast. A verdict for each sunrise and sunset, with reasons and a confidence label, based on Apple Weather data.
  • Alerts. Optional reminders before golden hour.
  • Camera and journal. Photos saved on your device with the sun, weather and place details, plus streaks, Monthly Recap and share cards.
  • Widgets and more. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, a Live Activity, Siri, Control Center controls and an Apple Watch app.
  • Sunlight Reports. An estimate of when direct sun reaches a place you choose.
