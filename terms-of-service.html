2. Description of Service

Sunblush is an iOS app that helps you anticipate, capture, and remember golden-hour moments. The Service provides:

  • Solar Intelligence — location-aware calculations of sunrise, sunset, golden hour, and blue hour.
  • Daily Capture — a minimal camera available during golden-hour windows for one-tap photos.
  • Memory & Reflection — a private on-device archive with metadata, streaks, and monthly recaps.
  • Widgets & Live Activity — home and lock screen countdowns and a Dynamic Island countdown during active golden-hour windows.

Additional features include Siri Shortcuts, weather tagging via WeatherKit, optional copying of captures to the iOS Photos library, and the optional Sunblush Pro subscription.
